# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Added page specifications
- **Support:** routes customers to tracking, a new requirement or a protected concern report.
- **Procurement:** makes proposal inputs and institutional routing explicit without promises.
- **Responsible AI:** sets workflow, data-boundary, evaluation and human-review expectations.
- **Accessibility:** describes current support and gives a direct assistance route.

All reuse the existing Flowly hero, card, CTA and bilingual patterns.
