# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## AI operating model
The assistant uses a constrained Flowly service catalog and deterministic fallback. Configurable provider modes are compatible chat completions, Dify and Bedrock. It has no action tools, never books meetings, and should not request credentials or confidential documents. Production implementations require use-case evaluation, human escalation and monitoring.
