# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Integration roadmap
| Integration | Trigger | Owner prerequisite |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar | Confirmed consultation | Calendar account and booking policy |
| Email | New request / status change | Transactional sender and templates |
| CRM | Qualified request | CRM schema and consent review |
| Monitoring | Health failure | Alert destination and on-call owner |

No integration is claimed as live until configured and tested end-to-end.
