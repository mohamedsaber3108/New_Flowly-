# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Implemented customer features
1. Service catalog and dedicated service pages.
2. Industry and government paths.
3. Guided needs discovery.
4. Requirements/contact form.
5. Consultation selection with provisional availability.
6. Reference-and-email request tracking.
7. AI service discovery assistant with deterministic fallback.
8. Privacy, terms, security, accessibility, responsible AI, procurement and support pages.
9. Owner-only team console with CSV export and controlled status updates.
10. Light/dark, Arabic/English, responsive and reduced motion support.

## Deferred by design
Account creation, billing, customer portal, CRM/calendar/email dispatch and externally verified case studies require selected providers, contracts and operating owners.
