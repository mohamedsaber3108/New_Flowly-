# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Decisions required from Flowly
1. Official contact email, legal entity name, address and support hours.
2. Target launch markets and governing privacy terms.
3. Confirmed service delivery capacity for each catalog category.
4. AI provider and data-processing position.
5. CRM, email and calendar providers.
6. EC2 account, domain, deployment owner, monitoring and backup retention.
7. Case studies and partner references approved for public use.
