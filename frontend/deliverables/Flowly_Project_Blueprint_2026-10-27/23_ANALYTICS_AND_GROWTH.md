# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Measurement design
No advertising tracker is currently activated. Before adding analytics: document purpose, consent model, retention, opt-out, event taxonomy and owner. Recommended events: service viewed, discovery started, request submitted, consultation requested, assistant guidance used, and request lookup completed. Do not send form contents or personal data to analytics.
