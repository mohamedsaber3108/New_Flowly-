# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Roadmap
**P0 before public launch:** contacts, operational owners, backups, legal review, support route, production environment and monitoring.

**P1:** CRM/email/calendar integration; multi-operator RBAC; audit-history UI; consent-aware analytics; case study approval process.

**P2:** customer portal, authenticated collaboration, project workspace, support SLAs, procurement document export and multilingual content expansion.
