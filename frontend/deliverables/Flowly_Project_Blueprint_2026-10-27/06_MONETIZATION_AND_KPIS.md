# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Commercial model
Discovery and solution implementation are scoped engagements. The proposal separates discovery, delivery, infrastructure, third-party licensing, model consumption and optional managed operations.

## Suggested KPIs
- Qualified request completion rate.
- Consultation request-to-confirmation rate.
- Time to first human response.
- Proposal-to-engagement conversion.
- Service category demand and public-sector intake volume.

No analytics tracker is activated in the current website. Add consent-aware measurement only after the analytics owner and privacy basis are approved.
