# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Personas and jobs
| Persona | Job to be done | First route |
|---|---|---|
| Business operator | Remove a costly workflow bottleneck | Find your solution |
| Executive sponsor | Decide whether to fund an AI/ERP program | Consultation |
| Technology lead | Assess integration, hosting and controls | Service detail → requirements |
| Public-sector institution | Start a governed institutional request | Government → institutional brief |
| Existing requester | Check progress without exposing data | Track a request |
| Procurement lead | Collect scope and commercial inputs | Procurement path |
