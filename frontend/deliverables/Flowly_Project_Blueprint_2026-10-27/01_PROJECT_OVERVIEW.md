# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Objective
Convert Flowly from a marketing presence into a credible first customer entry point for businesses and public institutions: discover a relevant capability, submit a structured need, request a provisional consultation, and follow progress safely.

## North-star outcome
A qualified visitor reaches a scoped human conversation with enough context to decide the next delivery step.

## Boundaries
This website is not an e-commerce checkout, an identity provider, a government system of record, or proof of certification/partnership. Any production solution is defined in a written project agreement.
