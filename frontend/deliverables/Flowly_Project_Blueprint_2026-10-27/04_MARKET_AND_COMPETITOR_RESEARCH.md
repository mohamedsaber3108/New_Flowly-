# Flowly Project Blueprint

**Prepared:** 27 October 2026  
**Scope:** Flowly bilingual AI, software and automation website, hosted preview and EC2 distribution.  
**Status key:** Implemented = present in the website or portable distribution; Planned = requires a real provider, legal review, commercial decision, or delivery engagement.

## Market patterns used
The existing research review benchmarked 20 official company sources: Accenture, IBM, McKinsey QuantumBlack, Thoughtworks, Deloitte, PwC, BCG, Capgemini, Cognizant, EPAM, Globant, Endava, Slalom, LeewayHertz, Intellectsoft, InData Labs, Turing, DataRobot, Dataiku and Palantir.

## Product decisions derived
- Lead with outcomes and a discovery path, not model names.
- Separate buyer paths for business and public institutions.
- Explain technology choice and ownership without claiming vendor partnerships.
- Use scoped proposals instead of unverified fixed prices or ROI claims.
